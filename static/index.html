1. Connect the keyboard

The plug marked OUT goes into MIDI IN. The plug marked IN goes into MIDI OUT.

  1. Turn on the keyboard.
  2. Connect the cable plug marked OUT to the keyboard's MIDI IN jack.
  3. Connect the cable plug marked IN to the keyboard's MIDI OUT jack.
  4. Connect the USB-C end to the Mac.
  5. Click "Find my keyboard". The app selects the "USB MIDI" device. macOS needs no driver and no extra program.

Most connection problems come from swapped plugs. If the test fails, swap the 2 plugs on the keyboard.

Do these settings on the keyboard:
  • Set PC mode to OFF. Hold the DEMO button for more than 1 second. Then select OFF.
  • Do not start a style or a song on the keyboard during practice.
  • Turn off DUAL and SPLIT.
  • Set TRANSPOSE to 0 and OCTAVE to 0.
  • Turn TOUCH on (press the TOUCH button).

2. Test the connection

Click "Start the test". Then press any key on your keyboard.

3. Find middle C

Find the 2 black keys near the middle of the keyboard. Middle C is the white key just left of them. The purple key below shows the place.

4. Soft and loud

Click "Start". Play 4 notes soft, then 4 notes loud.

5. Sound from the keyboard (you can skip this step)

The app plays a note on your keyboard. Did you hear it?
